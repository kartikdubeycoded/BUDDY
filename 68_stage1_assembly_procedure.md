# BUDDY Stage 1 Assembly Procedure

## 0. Configuration control
Record Git commit, OpenSCAD version, printer, material and slicer profile. Do not mix dimensions from different CAD revisions.

## 1. Calibration
Print File61 coupon. Measure holes, pins and sliding interfaces. Replace PROCESS values in File55 with the measured process compensation before committing structural parts.

## 2. Propulsion cartridge dimensional article
Manufacture the duct without powered rotors first. Measure 70 mm airway, 74 mm nominal OD, circularity and axial distortion. Reject any article whose distortion consumes the intended rotor/runout allowance.

## 3. Stator and rotor-envelope check
Install inert motor/rotor gauges or selected unpowered hardware. Rotate the full mechanical assembly manually. No contact is permitted anywhere in the complete revolution. Static CAD clearance is not sufficient evidence for powered operation.

## 4. Vane cartridges
Fit each cartridge independently. Move each through its full mechanical travel. Verify hard stops occur outside the intended commanded range and before any shell/duct/neighbor collision.

## 5. Service chassis
Fit cameras, battery carriers and electronics carriers. Preserve the protected airway and symmetric mass arrangement. Hardware protrusion beyond its CAD envelope requires a CAD revision, not force-fitting.

## 6. Wiring
Route power and signal wiring only through reserved corridors or documented revisions. Provide strain relief at moving/service interfaces. Keep loose wiring away from rotors and vane swept volumes.

## 7. Cage closure
Install lower cage, integrated core/chassis, upper cage and equatorial clamp. Alignment features must locate the halves without fasteners being used to force warped parts into position.

## 8. Inspection
Measure OD at multiple axes, airway ID, rotor clearance with gauges, vane travel, camera window alignment, service access, mass and XYZ CoG. Photograph and record each result.

## 9. Stage 1 mechanical acceptance
The inert assembly must close without interference, maintain the protected airway, permit service access, retain all modules, meet measured mass/CoG constraints or generate an explicit redesign action, and pass manual moving-interface checks.

Powered operation belongs to later guarded bench stages and requires selected hardware data plus the test campaign requirements.
